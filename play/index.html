Input options

Optional single-pointer mode: click a direction to keep moving, click it again to stop, and click Jump separately. Pause, leaving the page, and switching modes clear movement. Timing assist gives each jump the beat boost; it does not map voice input.

Both assists are optional and off by default. Opening options during play pauses the run; use Resume when ready. These controls do not establish MouthPad or VOX compatibility.

THE MECHANIC

Jump on the downbeat.

A timed jump carries farther than a rushed one. There’s no fail state—miss a ledge and you’ll find your footing again. The beat is a guide, not a test.

WHAT THIS IS

A first playable slice.

One original scene, one music-and-movement mechanic, and a finish. It’s an experimental browser build—not a Fortnite island, not an official Ren release, and not a validated playtest.

TRY IT YOUR WAY

Keyboard or touch.

Use the large on-screen controls on a phone or tablet. Sound is optional. The experience uses procedural canvas art and a tiny synthesized beat, with no external asset downloads.
