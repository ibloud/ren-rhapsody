# The Ledger — Cold-Player Playtest Protocol

**Target:** five cold players
**Build:** Ledger vertical slice v2
**Purpose:** determine whether the economic loop produces a meaningful player experience before expanding the ARG or moving the design into UEFN.

## Recruitment and setup

- Recruit **five players who have not been briefed on the intended strategy, ARG, or design hypothesis**.
- Use the same build for all sessions.
- Give only the minimum controls/instructions needed to start.
- Do not explain what an optimal choice is.
- Do not tell players that an ARG clue exists.
- Do not coach players toward opportunity-cost reasoning.

## Observation gates

### 0–90 seconds — orientation

Watch for:

- Do players understand what they are being asked to decide?
- Do they understand the immediate resources or constraints?
- Do they have a reason to continue after the first interaction?

### Decision point — deliberation

Record whether players:

- inspect more than one offer;
- compare time, operating exposure, and future opportunity;
- verbalize uncertainty or tradeoffs;
- select primarily from the largest visible payout.

The important distinction is **deliberation versus number optimization**, not whether the player makes the mathematically highest-value choice.

### End of session — player goal

Ask, without offering answer choices:

> **What were you trying to get better at?**

Then ask:

> **What made one choice harder than another?**

Do not supply terms such as “opportunity cost,” “precarity,” or “gig worker empathy” before the player answers.

### ARG observation

Record whether the player independently notices the ARG clue or follows its research path. Do not prompt or hint.

ARG discovery is a secondary signal for this gate. A player missing the clue does not by itself invalidate the build if the core decision loop is compelling.

## Evidence capture

For each session, record only what is needed for qualitative analysis:

- time to first meaningful decision;
- offers inspected;
- accept/decline sequence;
- observable deliberation;
- player-stated goal;
- player-stated emotional reaction, if volunteered;
- ARG discovery, if any;
- notable confusion or friction.

Do not collect financial credentials, payment information, or unnecessary personal data. No external transaction is required.

## Success signals for this gate

The build advances when the five sessions provide evidence that:

1. players can identify the decision they are making without a tutorial explanation;
2. at least some players deliberate over consequences rather than treating gross payout as the sole objective;
3. players can articulate a coherent improvement goal after play;
4. players describe the tradeoff in experiential terms, not only arithmetic terms;
5. ARG discovery can be evaluated separately from core-loop quality.

These are qualitative product-development signals, not statistically generalizable findings.

## Reporting

Publish findings as observations with counts and quotations only where permission exists. Do not present five-player results as representative of Fortnite players, gig workers, or the general public.
