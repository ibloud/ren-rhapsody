# Canonical Conceptual Architecture

**Status:** Canonical  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-24

The Ren Rhapsody economic architecture is not a set of parallel metaphors. It is a layered progression from valuation to accounting to human negotiation to systemic play.

## The canonical chain

**Money Game → The Ledger → The Weaver → Duet → 52 Cards of War → Veiled Dominion**

### Money Game — valuation

**Money Game asks what things cost.**

Its conceptual role is broader than literal cash. It establishes a multidimensional theory of value under constraint: material resources, information, reputation, innovation, governance, and labor can all function as things that are gained, spent, withheld, transferred, or made consequential.

### The Ledger — accounting

**The Ledger records the costs and consequences.**

Inside The Ren Rhapsody, the Money Game reference becomes a playable economic-survival system:

> **Offer → choice → cost → consequence → discovery**

The Ledger keeps gross income, direct costs, operating-cost proxies, time, opportunity cost, reputation, and unexpected events distinguishable. It is an accounting/game-state layer, not a casino or wagering system.

### The Weaver — negotiation and meaning

**The Weaver asks who gets to define value when two systems meet.**

The Weaver is the human and creative layer between independent systems. Its questions turn ledger categories into relationship questions:

- What does fair collaboration require?
- What happens when contributions are unequal?
- How are ownership and authorship separated?
- What changes when collaboration crosses jurisdictions?
- What happens when the relationship changes?
- What happens to the work after one participant dies?
- What does independence look like inside collaboration?

The Weaver therefore does **not** replace the Ledger. It interrogates it.

A Ledger can say:

> A decision costs Labor + Reputation + Governance.

The Weaver asks:

> Who decided those were the costs? Who bears them? Who benefits? Can both parties remain independent afterward?

### Duet — encounter

**Duet makes the negotiation between two systems concrete.**

The originating chain is preserved:

**Collaboration Proposal → The Weaver → Duet → 52 Cards of War → Veiled Dominion**

Duet is the experimental artifact in which the two systems actually meet.

### 52 Cards of War — systemic play

**52 Cards of War expands the encounter into a playable world/system.**

The economic and relational questions are no longer isolated to a single transaction. They can propagate through alliances, conflict, territory, identity, governance, and consequence.

### Veiled Dominion — sovereignty

**Veiled Dominion carries the resulting logic into sovereignty.**

The concern shifts from a single exchange or collaboration toward the larger question of who controls a system, how authority is constituted, and what survives when systems become institutions.

## Money Game ↔ Weaver correspondence

The Money Game value grammar can be read through the Weaver's human questions:

| Money Game dimension | Weaver question it exposes |
| --- | --- |
| **Capital** | Who contributes materially, and who receives value? |
| **Information** | Who knows what, who controls access, and who may disclose it? |
| **Reputation** | Whose identity and standing carry the collaboration? |
| **Innovation** | Who owns the thing that emerges from joint creation? |
| **Governance** | Who decides when the systems disagree? |
| **Labor** | How are unequal contributions recognized? |

This is a correspondence, not a claim that the two systems are identical.

## Design rule

**The Ledger does not become the Weaver.**

Keep the layers distinct:

- **Money Game = valuation**
- **Ledger = accounting**
- **Weaver = negotiation / meaning**
- **Duet = encounter**
- **52 Cards of War = systemic play**
- **Veiled Dominion = sovereignty**

This distinction prevents the same metaphor from being repeated across projects while preserving the shared mechanics underneath.

## Rights and provenance boundary

A reference to *Money Game*, Ren, the Weaver, Duet, or another named work/system does not establish participation, endorsement, ownership, permission, licensing, or partnership.

Economic evidence, creative/IP rights, and organizational participation remain separate provenance records.

For The Ren Rhapsody, the public-facing Zone 4 mechanic is **The Ledger**, not a playable casino. *Money Game* remains the narrative/artistic reference unless applicable rights and participation records establish otherwise.
