# Ren Rhapsody — Provenance, Participation, and Music Rights

> **Status:** Concept/prototype boundary. No Ren music rights are secured by this repository.

## What this repository is

*The Ren Rhapsody* is a fan-made interactive concept/prototype. The repository may describe desired music, characters, mechanics, and a possible UEFN implementation.

Those descriptions are not a license, collaboration agreement, endorsement, or evidence that the named artists have approved the project.

## Music boundary

Ren's recordings, compositions, lyrics, performances, branding, name, likeness, and collaborator works remain outside this repository's ownership unless a separate written agreement establishes otherwise.

A future implementation must identify the exact use before distributing music:

- soundtrack/background use;
- interactive or rhythm use;
- stems, edits, loops, or adaptations;
- trailer/promotional use;
- livestream/gameplay capture;
- educational or chart representations.

For each proposed track, keep separate records for the composition and the specific sound recording. Confirm the applicable rightsholders and authority before public use.

**Streaming availability is not a project license. Attribution is not a license. Noncommercial intent is not a license.**

## Participation boundary

References to Ren or other artists do not establish participation.

Project states should remain explicit:

- **Reference** — artistic/design research only.
- **Proposed** — collaboration or licensing conversation has been suggested.
- **Awaiting confirmation** — no authorization has been established.
- **Active** — written scope/participation terms exist.
- **Licensed** — written rights for specified uses exist.
- **Closed** — the proposal ended without authorization.

Do not represent a public profile, social post, fan interaction, or lack of objection as approval.

## Provenance packet

Before any public build containing third-party music or artist-controlled material, preserve:

1. exact track/recording/version;
2. composition writers and publisher/administrator where applicable;
3. master owner/authorized licensing contact;
4. requested use;
5. platform, territory, term, and exclusivity;
6. edits/loops/stems/chart data requested;
7. compensation;
8. credit;
9. executed permission/license;
10. approved final asset.

## Prototype rule

The safest prototype can prove the **mechanic** without distributing the copyrighted media.

Use placeholder or creator-owned audio while testing:

- beat synchronization;
- audio-reactive materials;
- parkour timing;
- interactive triggers;
- charting;
- player progression.

Replace the placeholder only after the rights record says the exact use is authorized.

**Principle:** The media file is not the rights record.


## Economic provenance cross-reference

The Ledger economic system uses the same provenance discipline as the music-rights boundary, but the records answer a different question: **why does this economic parameter exist?**

Economic parameters should preserve, at minimum:

- parameter identifier and definition;
- exact source and source type;
- publication/update date and retrieval date;
- geography and unit;
- applicable time period;
- transformation/calibration rule;
- confidence class and staleness status;
- fictional-game assumption, when applicable;
- relationship to the scenario/model rule that consumes it.

A source citation does not establish sponsorship, endorsement, participation, or rights. Likewise, a parameter record does not authorize use of third-party media. Keep **economic evidence**, **music/IP rights**, and **organizational participation** as separate records that may be linked by provenance identifiers.

For the Zone 4 migration, the public-facing mechanic is **The Ledger** rather than a playable casino. The *Money Game* trilogy remains a narrative/artistic reference only unless the applicable music and participation records establish otherwise.
